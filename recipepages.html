Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

These classic chocolate chip cookies are soft, chewy, and packed with chocolatey goodness. They are incredibly easy to bake and make the perfect treat for any occasion.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Sugar
  • Chocolate Chips

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Mix the dry ingredients in one bowl, and cream the butter and sugar in another before combining everything.
  3. Scoop the dough onto the baking sheet and bake for 10-12 minutes until golden brown.
