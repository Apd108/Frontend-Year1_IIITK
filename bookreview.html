My Book Reviews

A Thousand Splendid Suns

This profoundly moving novel by Khaled Hosseini spans decades of Afghan history through the lives of two women. It is a powerful story showing how the human spirit can endure unimaginable hardships.

The Silent Patient

This psychological thriller by Alex Michaelides is about a famous painter who shoots her husband and never speaks another word. The shocking twist at the end will leave you completely speechless.

Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone

A magical beginning to an unforgettable series written by J.K. Rowling. It beautifully captures a hidden world of wizards, witches, and the fight against dark magic.
